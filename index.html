About

I turn complex product problems into fast, accessible web experiences — and own them end to end, from data model and performance to the last pixel.

AI is part of how I ship

Claude CodeMCPAgentic workflows Prompt engineeringWorkflow automationReview guardrails

I don't just use AI — I define how a team uses it. At cargo.one I set the guardrails for agentic development (Claude Code, MCP) across 20 engineers: where AI accelerates implementation, review and testing, and where a human still owns the design. The result is measurably faster feature cycles without giving up rigor.

Day to day that's prompt-driven scaffolding, MCP integrations that wire AI into our real workflow, and automation that removes the repetitive parts of shipping — so the team spends its time on the problems that actually need a human.
