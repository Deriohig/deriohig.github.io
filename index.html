On cargo.one's AI team I built an import-procurement flow, now in internal evaluation. It extracts shipment details from forwarders' emails, requests rates from overseas partners, parses their replies and assembles a door-to-door quote. It's a multi-agent Pydantic AI workflow on Gemini and Claude. A person reviews every quote before it is sent, and the evals library I co-built (gold-set and LLM-as-judge checks, traced in Pydantic Logfire) runs in CI for most of the team's agents.

Before that I built Claude Code skills and an MCP server that about 20 engineers on 5 teams now use, with a person reviewing every change.
